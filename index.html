Fruit Merge Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you play Fruit Merge.

2. Information Collection

Fruit Merge does not require users to create an account to play. The game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect information such as advertising IDs, IP addresses, device information, app interactions, and diagnostic data, depending on device settings, consent, and applicable services.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to display advertisements, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, diagnose technical issues, and provide personalized advertising where permitted.

4. Third-Party Services

Fruit Merge uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google. Google's privacy policy is available at Google Privacy Policy. Information about Google's advertising technologies is available at Google Advertising Technologies.

5. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by Google and its service providers to deliver advertisements, measure performance, prevent fraud, and operate their services. The developer does not sell users' personal information.

6. Data Retention and Security

Data collected by third-party services is subject to their retention and security practices. The developer does not control data independently collected by those services.

7. Children's Privacy

Fruit Merge's handling of children's data and advertising must comply with applicable privacy laws and Google Play requirements. The game's actual audience and advertising configuration determine the applicable requirements.

8. Your Choices

Depending on your device and location, you may be able to manage advertising personalization and advertising identifiers through your device settings or Google's privacy controls.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.
